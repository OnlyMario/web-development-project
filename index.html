BUILD A BALANCED PLATE

Include protein, carbohydrates, healthy fats, and fruits or vegetables throughout your day. Portions depend on your activity, preferences, and goals.

🥩

LEAN PROTEIN

Helps muscles repair and grow.

  • Chicken or turkey breast
  • Lean ground beef
  • Salmon, tuna, or shrimp
  • Eggs and egg whites
  • Low-fat Greek yogurt
  • Tofu and tempeh
🍚

CARBOHYDRATES

Fuel workouts and daily activity.

  • Brown or white rice
  • Oatmeal
  • Potatoes
  • Whole-grain bread
  • Beans and lentils
  • Pasta and tortillas
🥑

HEALTHY FATS

Support overall health.

  • Avocados
  • Olive oil
  • Almonds and cashews
  • Peanut or almond butter
  • Chia and flax seeds
  • Dark chocolate
🥦

FRUITS & VEGGIES

Provide fiber, vitamins, and minerals.

  • Broccoli and spinach
  • Carrots and bell peppers
  • Asparagus and zucchini
  • Apples and bananas
  • Berries and oranges
  • Watermelon

T MUSCLE'S NUTRITION BASICS

💧 Drink water regularly throughout the day.

💪 Include protein in your meals.

🍚 Eat enough to support training and recovery.

🥗 Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.

😴 Get enough sleep and allow recovery time.

⚖️ For fat loss, a sustainable calorie deficit can help. For muscle gain, adequate food and progressive training matter.
